HOW TO PLAY

1 · BUILDTap the bins. Most orders are sauce + cheese plus the extras on the customer's ticket. Seven extras: pepperoni, mushroom, olive, pepper, anchovy, onion, jalapeño. From day 2, named specialty pies (+20 pay) appear — copy the ticket exactly, and watch out: the BIANCA skips the sauce and the MARINARA skips the cheese.

2 · BAKETap the oven to load it, tap again to pull. The bake bar runs gray (raw) → green (good) → gold (perfect, +25) → amber (overdone but servable) → red (burnt). Raw and burnt pizzas get refused.

3 · SERVETap the customer whose ticket matches. Wrong orders bounce — trash mistakes and start over.

EARNFast serves tip up to +50. Back-to-back serves build a combo that multiplies tips up to x2. Gold VIPs pay double but won't wait around. Orange-border RUSH customers pay 1.5× base but their patience drains twice as fast. Once a day, HAPPY HOUR doubles all tips for 12 seconds. From day 6 the silver-ticket CRITIC may visit: only a perfect bake will do — flat 200 pay and a rave review restores a heart.

SURVIVEA walkout costs a heart — lose them all and you're closed. Clearing a day's quota restores one.
